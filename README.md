# Features added after the bootcamp

### Mobile support
An on-screen jump button appears automatically on touch devices.

### Better jump feel
- variable jump height (tap for a short hop, hold for a full jump)
- a short grace window to jump after leaving a platform 
- an early jump press still registers 

### Enemies and combat
Goombas patrol back and forth on solid ground. Jump on one from above to stomp it for points; touch it from the side and you take a hit instead. You can tank upto 3 hits.

### Power-ups
Some question blocks drop a mushroom (extra life) or a star (temporary invincibility and a speed boost) instead of a coin. They fall and land on a platform.

### Combo scoring
Stomping multiple enemies in a row without touching the ground builds a combo multiplier for bonus points. It resets the moment you land.

### Better graphics/sounds added
- the sky, ground, and accent colors shift every few thousand pixels, cycling through a meadow, desert, dusk, and night look.
- hills, clouds, and a sun scroll at different speeds behind the action for a sense of depth.
- small particles show up when you collect coins, break blocks, stomp enemies, and jump.
- sound effects created on the fly by the prog (there's a mute button in the top corner)

### Pause menu
Press P or Escape, or tap the on-screen button.

### Stats
- hi-score (can be reset)
- coins
- best combo
